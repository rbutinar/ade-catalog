Discover, organize, and put your
data assets to work.

ade-catalog helps you discover and organize the data assets scattered across your organization, then puts them to direct use — browsable in the app, and through integrations with the platforms and LLMs your teams already work with — supporting data discovery, assessment, and ongoing monitoring.

See how it works ↓

What you get

Cross-platform lineage

Discover assets across platforms and trace where data comes from and where it's used — end to end, across tool boundaries.

AI-assisted, human-reviewed

Generated descriptions you can review and edit — human edits are protected and never silently overwritten on re-scan.

A catalog assistant

Ask questions in plain language and get answers grounded in your own metadata, with links straight to the objects.

Open integrations

An open MCP interface plugs the catalog into the LLMs and tools your teams already use.

Part of the ADE family

Independent, composable tools for agentic data engineering — use either on its own or together. ade-catalog maps what exists and how it's connected; ade-ops operates what you run and where you deploy it. Map the territory, then operate it.
